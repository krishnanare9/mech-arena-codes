Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

MechNova is an independent fan resource for Mech Arena promo-code updates, rewards, packs and gameplay. This policy explains the information that may be processed when you use this website.

Information and website operation

Visitors can browse public pages without creating a MechNova account. The website uses Firebase services to load and manage public promo-code and site-setting information. The private admin area uses Firebase Authentication to verify authorized administrators; authentication information is handled by Firebase and is not displayed publicly.

Do not submit passwords, one-time codes, payment-card details, or other sensitive information through public website fields or social channels.

Cookies and third-party services

MechNova uses Firebase services to operate public website features and its restricted administrator area. When you visit embedded content or follow external links, third-party providers such as YouTube, Discord, Instagram, and Amazon may process technical information according to their own privacy policies. MechNova does not control those providers' cookies or data practices.

MechNova does not currently state that Google Analytics or Google AdSense advertising is active. If Google AdSense or another advertising or analytics service is enabled, this policy will be updated to identify the service and explain relevant data use and visitor choices. You can manage cookies through your browser settings; blocking some cookies or scripts may affect third-party features.

Affiliate links and advertising

MechNova currently includes Amazon India affiliate links in its Gaming Gear Picks. As an Amazon Associate, MechNova earns from qualifying purchases. If you purchase through an affiliate link, MechNova may receive a commission at no additional cost to you. Affiliate links are identified near relevant products. Google AdSense or other display advertising will be shown only after the required provider approval and technical integration are complete; a settings toggle alone does not mean ads are active.

Data sharing, retention and security

MechNova does not sell visitor information. Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the website, or disclosed where legally required. Firebase access controls are used for administrator functions; however, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children and external websites

This website is a general gaming information resource and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. External websites are governed by their own policies, and MechNova is not responsible for their content or practices.

Policy updates and contact

This policy may be revised as website features change. The current version will be published on this page. For privacy questions, contact MechNova through the official Instagram or Discord.
