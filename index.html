📘 How to Redeem a Promo Code

Choose the steps for your device. The game's menus and promotion rules can change, so follow the current in-game instructions.
🖥️

PC & Android

Redeem from the game when the Promo Codes option is available.
  1. Open Mech Arena and sign in to the account that will receive the reward.
  2. Open your Inbox, then choose Promo Codes.
  3. Paste an eligible code and confirm it.
  4. Check the result and claim the reward from your in-game Inbox.
📱

iPhone & iPad

Some iOS redemptions continue in the official activation page.
  1. In Mech Arena, open your Inbox and tap Promo Codes.
  2. If the game opens an activation page, enter your Player ID and the code there.
  3. Confirm the redemption, return to the game and check your Inbox for the reward.
New Player codes may have a short redemption window, often the first 24 hours after account creation. Check each code’s eligibility and any campaign-specific rules. Only enter account details on an official page opened by the game; never share your password or OTP.

The difference between New Player and All Player promo codes

Mech Arena has two main types of promo codes.

NEW PLAYER

Promo Codes

Available only for newly created accounts. Most need to be redeemed within the first 24 hours after registration.

  • ♟Only for eligible new accounts
  • ◷Check the offer window; often the first 24 hours
  • ⬡Usually one New Player code per account
  • ★May include Mechs, A-Coins, Pilots, weapons or other resources
VS

ALL PLAYER

Promo Codes

Can be redeemed by new and existing accounts while the code remains active and the account is eligible.

  • ♟♟Available to new and existing accounts
  • 🎁Different codes can be redeemed over time
  • ◷Generally one promo code every 24 hours
  • ∞Often Credits, Pilot Marks, Implant Parts or Skins
✦
Important to know

Codes must still be active to work. Rewards are delivered to your in-game Inbox. Claim them before the reward message expires.

🎁
?1. How do I redeem Mech Arena promo codes?⌃

There are two official ways to redeem promo codes.

▣ On PC / Android (inside the game)

  1. Launch Mech Arena and log in to your account.
  2. Open your Inbox using the envelope icon in the top-right corner.
  3. Select Promo Codes.
  4. Copy and paste an active code, then confirm.
  5. If accepted, check your in-game Inbox for the reward.
OR

● On iPhone / iPad (iOS)

On iOS, tapping Promo Codes in the Inbox may open the official activation page in your browser.

Enter your Player ID and promo code there, confirm, then return to the game and check for the reward.

✦ Important

New Player promo codes often need to be activated within the first 24 hours after account creation. Some promotions may also have extra level or account requirements.

?2. Where can I find new Mech Arena codes?⌄

Promo codes have no fixed release schedule. They may appear during game updates, seasonal events, anniversaries or official campaigns. Check the current code list, eligibility notes and any recorded check date because codes can expire or have account limits.

?3. Why is my promo code not working?⌄

A code may be expired, limited to another account type, already redeemed, entered incorrectly or temporarily blocked by a redemption cooldown. Campaign limits or account requirements can also apply. Copy the code exactly and check its eligibility notes before trying again.

?4. Why haven’t I received rewards after redeeming a code?⌄

Confirm the game accepted the code, then check your in-game Inbox and claim the reward message. It may take a few minutes to appear. If it is still missing, verify the code was active and your account met the campaign rules, then contact official in-game support. Never share your password or OTP.

?5. Can I redeem more than one promo code?⌄

You can redeem different eligible codes over time, but generally only one promo code can be used every 24 hours. New Player codes are usually limited to one per eligible account. Follow the game’s current message if its limits change.

?6. How often are new codes released?⌄

There is no guaranteed schedule. New codes may be tied to major updates, seasonal promotions, anniversaries, creator campaigns or community events. Availability and expiry can vary, so check the current list before redeeming.
