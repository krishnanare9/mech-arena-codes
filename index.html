The difference between New Player and All Player promo codes

Mech Arena has two main types of promo codes.

NEW PLAYER Promo Codes

Available only for newly created accounts. They usually need to be redeemed within the first 24 hours after registration.

  • Only for new accounts
  • Usually redeem within 24 hours of registration
  • Only one New Player code per account
  • May include Mechs, A-Coins, Pilots, weapons and other resources

ALL PLAYER Promo Codes

Can be redeemed by new and existing accounts while the code remains active.

  • Available to new and existing accounts
  • Multiple different codes may be redeemed over time
  • Only one promo code every 24 hours
  • Often reward Credits, Pilot Marks, Implant Parts or Skins

Important to know

  • Codes must still be active to work.
  • Rewards are delivered to your in-game Inbox.
  • Claim rewards before the message expires.
